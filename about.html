OUR JOURNEY

History and Background

The Department of Mathematics was established as part of the university's commitment to advancing higher education, research and professional training. Since its establishment, the department has grown in response to emerging academic, technological and societal needs.

Through the development of academic programmes, expansion of research activities and strengthening of partnerships, the department continues to deliver quality mathematical education and develop graduates equipped for scientific, technological and professional careers.

VISION

Our Vision

To be a leading centre of mathematical education, innovation and research, producing competent, ethical and globally competitive graduates who contribute to sustainable development.

MISSION

Our Mission

To provide quality education, promote mathematical research and foster professional development through effective teaching, practical training, collaboration and community engagement.

PRINCIPLES THAT GUIDE US

Our Core Values

Integrity

We uphold honesty, transparency, accountability and ethical conduct in academic, research and administrative activities.

Excellence

We strive for high standards in teaching, learning, research and service through continuous improvement and dedication.

Innovation

We encourage critical thinking, creativity and mathematical solutions for emerging scientific and societal challenges.

KNOWLEDGE AND DISCOVERY

Research Interests

The department advances mathematical knowledge through inquiry, scholarly collaboration and practical applications across scientific and professional fields.

Pure Mathematics

Algebra, analysis, geometry and number theory.

Applied Mathematics

Mathematical modelling for engineering, science and industry.

Statistics

Statistical inference, data analysis and experimental design.

Computational Mathematics

Numerical methods, scientific computing and mathematical software.
