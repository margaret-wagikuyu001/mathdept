OUR JOURNEY

History And Background

The Department of Mathematics was established as part of the university's commitment to advancing higher education, research, and professional training. Since its establishment, the department has continued to grow in response to emerging academic, technological, and societal needs. Through the development of academic programmes, expansion of research activities, and strengthening of partnerships with industry and other institutions, the department has progressively enhanced its capacity to deliver quality education and contribute to national and global development. Today, it remains committed to academic excellence, innovation, knowledge creation, and the development of skilled graduates equipped to address contemporary challenges.

Our Vision

To be a leading centre of academic excellence, innovation, and research, recognized for producing competent, ethical, and globally competitive graduates who contribute to sustainable development and societal transformation.

Our Mission

To provide quality and transformative education, promote innovative research, and foster professional development through effective teaching, practical training, collaboration, and community engagement, while nurturing graduates equipped with the knowledge, skills, and values necessary to address contemporary societal and global challenges.
