Past Papers and Notes

Student are able to get past papers from a site called Studyroom .The site was created to help students :

  • Past Papers & Notes: Search and filter past papers, lecture notes, and study materials by course, category, university, or keyword. Support has also expanded to TVET and CDACC study materials.

  • Offline Access: Download materials for offline reading and revision

  • AI Study Tools: Generate flashcards, smart notes, and practice quizzes, or use AI scanning/chat tools to get help with questions and concepts.

  • Organization & PDF Tools: Create study lists, set reminders and timetables, and view, annotate, merge, or convert PDFs.

  • Extra Campus Features: Listings highlight additional features such as a student marketplace (with M-Pesa integration), campus updates, and TechLab support
