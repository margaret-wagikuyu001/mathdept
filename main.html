UPCOMING SEMINARS

Departmental Mathematics Seminars

Regular seminars featuring mathematics talks ,student presentations and current research topics

Each session begins with a 45-minute talk by a lecturer,a guest speaker or a senior student,folloed by a short question and answer period. students are encouraged to present their own projects.Topics change each week, so check the events page for the schedule.

  • When:Every Friday,2.00-3.00PM
  • Where:Lecture Hall 2, Mathematics block
  • Who:Faculty,guest speakers,students
  • Topics:Algebra,analysis,statistics,applied maths
  • Entry:Free,no registration
