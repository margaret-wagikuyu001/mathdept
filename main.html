LATEST UPDATES

Department News

Annual University Mathematics Competition

Registration is open for the annual university mathematics competition. The competition is open to all undergraduate students, divided into a first and second-year category and a third year and above category.

Registration deadline: Friday, 5 December 2026. Register at the department office or by emailing the competition coordinator.

Examination Timetable and Notices

The end-of-semester examination timetable will be published on the department notice board and the student portal. Students should check the timetable and report clashes to the department office as early as possible.

Guest Lecture Series

The department will host guest speakers from industry and other universities. Topics include data science, cryptography and careers for mathematics graduates. Attendance is free and open to students and staff.

Seminars and Workshops

UPCOMING SEMINAR

Departmental Mathematics Seminars

Regular seminars feature mathematics talks, student presentations and current research topics.

  • When: Every Friday, 2:00-3:00 PM
  • Where: Lecture Hall 2, Mathematics Block
  • Who: Faculty, guest speakers and students
  • Topics: Algebra, analysis, statistics and applied mathematics
  • Entry: Free; no registration
MATHEMATICS WORKSHOP

Problem-Solving and Computing Workshop

Practical workshops on mathematical problem-solving, proof methods and computational tools.

  • When: Second Saturday monthly, 10:00 AM-1:00 PM
  • Where: Computer Lab 1
  • Tools: Python and MATLAB
  • Level: Undergraduate students, all years
  • Registration: By email; limited seats
